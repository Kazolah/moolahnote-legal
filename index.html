Moolahnote privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-01. Contact: kyawzinlatt94@gmail.com

Moolahnote is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what it collects, why, who handles it, how long it is kept and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • From Google when you sign in: your name and email address.
  • After you connect an account through Plaid: the institution name, account names and last four digits, and your transactions (date, amount, currency, merchant and category).
  • What you add yourself: account nicknames, categories, descriptions and notes.
  • Technical logs that contain identifiers and counts. They do not contain your bank data.

We never see or store your bank username or password. You enter them only inside Plaid. We do not store full card numbers.

Why we use it

Only to show your transactions and totals on your calendar, keep them in sync and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers. The app has no third-party analytics or ad tracking.

Who handles your data

  • Plaid connects to your bank and passes us your transactions. Plaid's own notice applies to what it collects: plaid.com/legal.
  • Google signs you in (name and email only).
  • Amazon Web Services stores our data in Canada (ca-central-1).

No one else receives your data unless the law requires it.

Consent

Before connecting an account we show you what is collected and ask for your agreement. We record when you agreed and which version of the text you saw. Plaid shows its own consent screen too. You can withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting the bank in the app.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Plaid access tokens are encrypted again with a key tied to your household and are kept only on our server, never on your phone. Access is limited by role, and the cloud account that runs the service is protected with multi-factor authentication.

How long we keep it

While you use Moolahnote we keep your transactions so your history stays available. If you disconnect a bank you choose whether to keep or delete its transactions. If you delete your account in the app (Settings, then Delete account), your bank connections, accounts, transactions, settings and sign-in are removed right away, and the bank connections are revoked at Plaid. A small marker with no personal data stops that sign-in from recreating the account and expires after two days. Backups age out within 35 days. Operational logs are deleted after 14 days.

Your choices and rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data and withdraw consent. In the app you can rename or exclude accounts, disconnect a bank (with delete) and delete your account. For anything else, email the contact above. Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) you can also ask what we hold about you and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Moolahnote reads your accounts. It never moves money.
